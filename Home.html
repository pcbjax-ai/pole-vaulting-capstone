Pole Vaulting in Action

What Is Pole Vaulting?

Pole vaulting is a track and field event where an athlete uses a long, flexible pole made of fiber glass to jump over a cross bar.

Basic Technique

Approach

The athlete sprints down the runway, building speed before the plant.

Plant and Takeoff

The pole is planted into the box as the athlete hurdles off the ground.

Swing & Inversion

The vaulter swings their legs and inverts their body to gain height.

Bar Clearance

The athlete turns over the bar and releases the pole before landing safely.

Equipment

  • Pole: Usually made of fiberglass or carbon fiber
  • Standards: Hold the crossbar at adjustable heights
  • Landing Pit: Large foam mats for landings

Pole Vault Rules at a Track Meet

Attempts

A vaulter is allowed three attempts at each height. Knocking the bar off or failing to clear it counts as a miss.

Bar Heights

The bar height gets higher throughout the competition.

Time Limit

Vaulters have a limited amount of time to make an attempt.

How to Win

The winner is whoever clears the highest height. If there is a tie, the winner is decided based on who had less misses.
